The house

A bakery with a café in the Eixample

Breakfast is at the window counter, on the bench under the slogan on the wall, or out on the terrace under the Gran Vía’s trees. They open at 7:30 every day.

  • From their own bakery

    Cakes and pastries made in-house — on Instagram they show, step by step, how their San Marcos cake is made — and a case of pastries, empanadillas and pizza from first thing.

  • Your bread, your toast

    Toast comes on white or seeded bread; for €0.20 more, on spelt-and-walnut, rye or sourdough bread.

  • Orders and takeaway

    Call, place your order and pick it up: it will be ready when you arrive. And if you can’t stay, they’ll pack it to take away.

From the bakery

The morning display case

Butter croissants, ensaimadas, napolitanas and empanadillas on pine trays with their zigzag labels; next to them, cakes and sweets. Every photo was taken by their customers.

The café

Pine, cubes and the Gran Vía

Pine walls with their slogan, a cube-pattern tiled floor, a marble counter and a big window onto the avenue. Outside, the terrace under the trees.
